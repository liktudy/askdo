# Ask To Result Flow

## Purpose

Convert a user ask into a result through kit reuse, kit build, flow execution, and meaningful feedback.

## Flow

1. Receive the ask.
2. Analyze ask completeness, feasibility, and closure.
3. Separate facts, assumptions, inferences, and unknowns when the ask is substantial.
4. Verify current or specific facts before relying on them when verification is needed.
5. Identify the scenario planning basis: objective, scope, audience, result artifact, inputs, constraints, assumptions, risk tolerance, execution depth, success criteria, likely kit path, and closure logic.
6. If the planning basis is incomplete, infeasible, not logically closed, conflicting, or materially undecided, present the planning issues and ask the decision-maker how to handle them.
7. Resolve the scenario planning gate through user choice: confirm planning frame, revise planning frame, resolve now, use defaults, defer non-blocking planning items, or stop.
8. Do not generate recommended scenario options until the scenario planning gate is resolved.
9. Generate 1 to 3 complete scenario options with outcomes, assumptions, planning decisions applied, likely kit path, expected artifacts, risk notes, and closure logic.
10. Ask the user to choose one scenario before build or execution.
11. Check existing kits before building a new kit.
12. Reuse a kit when it naturally fits the selected scenario.
13. Build a new kit only when the selected scenario has a reusable business capability boundary.
14. Check approval, boundary, and permission requirements.
15. Run the selected kit flow.
16. Produce the result.
17. Verify generated outputs and claims before delivery.
18. Record meaningful level signals only when they improve reuse, lifecycle, or performance.

## Hard Rules

- Do not bypass approval.
- Do not generate recommended scenario options before the scenario planning gate is resolved.
- Do not build or run before the ask refinement gate, scenario planning gate, and scenario selection gate are resolved.
- Do not invent facts, citations, cases, dates, numbers, or product features.
- Do not hide uncertainty.
- Do not delete user-owned kits.
- Do not create process logs by default.
- Do not expose heavy internal organization language in user-facing onboarding.
